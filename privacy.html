Privacy Policy: The PT Home Plan

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The PT Home Plan ("the app") is made by The Spectrum Tech Engine. It helps you organise the exercise sheets your physiotherapist gives you. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The app is an organisation tool, not medical advice. Always check everything with your GP or physiotherapist.

What stays on your phone

  • Your exercise plans, advice notes and goals
  • Which exercises you've ticked off, how sessions felt, and your streaks
  • Your settings and reminder times
  • Pictures of exercises cropped from your sheet photos

These are stored only on your device (in the website version, only in your browser on that device). If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your own Google backup so they can be restored on a new phone.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Sheet photos, while being read. When you scan a sheet, the photos are sent to Google's Gemini AI (through Google Firebase) to read the exercises. Neither we nor the app keep a copy on any server. Under Google's free service terms, Google may use these requests to improve its products. Please crop out or cover your name, date of birth or any patient numbers before scanning.
  • Your username. The app creates an anonymous account (no email or password) with Google Firebase and stores the username you choose with it.
  • Anonymous usage numbers. To help improve the app, it counts a few things, like how often it is opened and how many sessions are finished. These are numbers only, linked to a random ID, never your plans, exercises, photos or name.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they're scheduled on your phone. No reminder information is sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone number, address or real name.

Deleting your information

Settings → Delete everything and start again removes all your plans, progress and pictures from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes them. To have your username record deleted from Firebase, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+).
